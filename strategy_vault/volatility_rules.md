# MASTER RULE: VOLATILITY TRADING
When evaluating high-volatility micro-cap stocks (like HIVE or FATN), apply the following strict criteria:
1. Do not buy if the RSI is above 70, no matter how bullish the news is.
2. If the news mentions "dilution", "secondary offering", or "warrants", automatically assign a bearish score of -0.8 or lower.
3. If the news mentions a "partnership" with a Fortune 500 company, assign a high conviction bullish score.
Always prioritize capital preservation in high-RVOL environments.
